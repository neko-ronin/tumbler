Flight moves · B / Shift

Hold B (Shift) for more roll authority. Alternate left/right stick for linked wingovers; hold a side for a descending spiral. Release B and centre the stick to recover.

Push forward to dive and build speed, then pull progressively to swoop back up. Height comes from that speed. The motor wing uses a gentler bank envelope.

LT / RT pull independent handles. Right stick looks; Y / V changes view. After a safe landing, press X / E to unhook and walk. A / Space jumps: stand still for a celebration, heel click or star; move for a skip. X / E hooks back in; release, then A / Space launches from there. Gliders need downhill ground or lift to stay up.
