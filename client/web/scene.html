Flight moves · B / Shift

Hold B (Shift) for more roll authority. Alternate left/right stick for linked wingovers; hold a side for a descending spiral. Release B and centre the stick to recover. Sudden control changes can swing the pilot under the wing; ease the controls to let it settle.

Parakite: forward / W raises both handles to dive; back / S pulls them to flare. Centre returns to best glide. Steering combines weight shift and outside-handle release; at full release, steering uses weight shift alone.

Paraglider and motor wing: forward / W applies the speedbar; back / S brakes. Steering assists weight shift with inside brake. Build speed, then pull progressively to swoop back up; height comes from that speed. Motor power uses LB/RB or R/F and takes time to spool.

LT / RT pull independent handles. Right stick looks; Y / V changes view. After landing, press X / E to unhook and walk. X / E hooks back in; release, then A / Space starts assisted wing inflation and a takeoff run. Keep clear ground ahead. Gliders need downhill ground or lift to take off. X / E cancels preparation and unhooks.

On foot, A / Space jumps: stand still for a celebration, heel click or star; move for a skip. V / right-stick click changes between first person and chase view. Y / F draws or sheathes the katana. Menu changes weather.
